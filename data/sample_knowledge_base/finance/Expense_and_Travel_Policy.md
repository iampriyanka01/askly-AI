# Expense and Travel Policy

This policy sets out what business expenses Acme Corp reimburses and how to claim them. All expense claims are submitted in Concur (expenses.company.com). Questions go to expenses@company.com.

## 1 General Principles
Employees should spend company money as carefully as their own. Expenses must be necessary for business, reasonable and supported by an itemised receipt. Expenses for personal entertainment, fines and penalties, and alcohol without a client present are not reimbursed.

## 2 Submitting an Expense Claim

### 2.1 How to Submit
Log in to Concur, select "Create New Report", add each expense with its date, category and business purpose, and attach a photo or PDF of the itemised receipt. Receipts can be captured with the Concur mobile app. Submit the report for approval by your line manager.

### 2.2 Deadlines
Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed except in exceptional circumstances approved by the Finance Director.

### 2.3 Reimbursement Timing
Approved claims are paid into your salary bank account within 10 working days of manager approval. You can track the status of each report in Concur.

### 2.4 Missing Receipts
If a receipt is lost, complete the "Missing Receipt Declaration" in Concur. Missing receipt declarations are accepted for amounts up to USD 75 and no more than three times per year.

## 3 Approval Limits
Line managers approve expense reports up to USD 2,500. Reports between USD 2,500 and USD 10,000 also need approval from the department head, and reports above USD 10,000 need approval from the Finance Director.

## 4 Business Travel

### 4.1 Booking Travel
All flights, hotels and rail travel must be booked through the corporate travel tool, Navan (travel.company.com), at least 14 days before departure where possible. Bookings made outside Navan are only reimbursed if they were at least 15% cheaper.

### 4.2 Flights
Economy class is used for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more, and business class for flights over 10 hours with department head approval.

### 4.3 Hotels
Nightly hotel limits are USD 250 in tier-1 cities (New York, London, San Francisco, Singapore, Tokyo) and USD 180 elsewhere, excluding taxes.

### 4.4 Meals and Per Diem
Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to USD 75 per day in total. Alternatively, employees may claim a per diem of USD 60 per day without receipts for domestic travel.

### 4.5 Mileage
Use of a personal car for business is reimbursed at USD 0.67 per mile. Commuting between home and your usual office is not reimbursable.

## 5 Corporate Cards
Employees who travel more than four times per year can apply for a corporate card through Concur. Corporate card transactions must be reconciled in Concur within 30 days. Personal use of the corporate card is not permitted.

## 6 Home Office Equipment
Remote and hybrid employees may claim up to USD 500 once every 3 years for home office equipment such as a desk, chair or monitor. Use the expense category "Home Office" and attach the receipt.
