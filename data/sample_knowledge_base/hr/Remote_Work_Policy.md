# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## 1 Work Arrangements
Acme Corp offers three work arrangements: Office-based (4 or more days per week in the office), Hybrid (2 to 3 days per week in the office, with Tuesday and Thursday as team anchor days) and Remote (no regular office attendance, approved for specific roles). Your arrangement is recorded in your employment contract and in the HR Portal under My Profile > Work Arrangement.

## 2 Requesting a Change of Arrangement
Employees who have completed probation may request a different arrangement through HR Portal > My Profile > Work Arrangement > Request Change. The line manager responds within 10 working days. Approval depends on the role, team needs and performance.

## 3 Working From Another Country
Employees may work from a country other than their country of employment for up to 20 working days per calendar year, with manager approval and a completed "Work Abroad" request in the HR Portal at least 15 working days before travel. Longer stays require approval from HR and Legal because of tax and immigration rules. Working from sanctioned countries is never permitted.

## 4 Home Office Setup
Remote and hybrid employees must have a safe, quiet workspace and a reliable internet connection of at least 25 Mbps. Equipment can be claimed under the Expense and Travel Policy, Section 6 (up to USD 500 every 3 years). Company laptops must not be used by family members.

## 5 Security When Working Remotely
Always connect through the VPN when accessing internal systems from outside the office. Lock your screen when you step away. Do not work on confidential documents in public places where your screen can be seen, and never use public computers for company work.

## 6 Internet Allowance
Remote employees receive a monthly internet allowance of USD 40, paid automatically with salary. Hybrid employees are not eligible.
