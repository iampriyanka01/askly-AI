# New Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Acme Corp! This guide walks you through your first days and weeks. Your onboarding buddy and your manager are your first points of contact; for anything else, ask Askly or email onboarding@company.com.

## 1 Before Your First Day
You will receive a welcome email about one week before you start, with your work email address and instructions to complete your personal details, bank details and tax forms in the HR Portal. Your laptop is shipped to your home address (remote employees) or handed over at reception on day one (office-based employees).

## 2 Your First Day

### 2.1 Setting Up Your Laptop and Accounts
Switch on your laptop and connect to the internet. Sign in with your work email and the temporary password sent to your personal email. You will be asked to choose a new password (see the Account Security Policy for the rules) and to set up two-factor authentication with Microsoft Authenticator. After sign-in, required applications (Outlook, Teams, Slack, 1Password, GlobalProtect) install automatically within about 30 minutes.

### 2.2 Day-One Checklist
On your first day: complete laptop and account setup; join the 10:00 New Starter Welcome session (calendar invite sent to your work email); meet your onboarding buddy; collect your building access badge from reception (office-based employees); and review your 30-60-90 day plan with your manager.

## 3 Your First Week

### 3.1 Mandatory Training
The following courses in the Learning Hub (learn.company.com) must be completed within your first 14 days: Information Security Essentials, Data Privacy and GDPR, Code of Conduct, and Anti-Harassment. Managers of people also complete Inclusive Leadership within 30 days.

### 3.2 Benefits Enrolment
Enrol in benefits (health insurance dependants, retirement plan contributions) in the HR Portal under Benefits within 30 days of your start date. If you do not enrol within 30 days, you will have to wait for the next open enrolment period in November.

### 3.3 Tools and Channels
Day-to-day communication happens in Slack. Join #general, #announcements, your team channel and #ask-it for quick IT questions. Meetings use Microsoft Teams. Documents live in Google Drive and the internal wiki (wiki.company.com).

## 4 Your First 90 Days

### 4.1 Probation
Your first 90 days are a probation period. Your manager will hold check-ins at 30 and 60 days and a probation review in week 11. See the HR Handbook Section 7.2.

### 4.2 Requesting Access to Systems
Access to team-specific systems (for example Salesforce, Jira, AWS or finance systems) is requested in the IT Service Portal under Request > Access. Your manager approves each request, and most access is granted within 1 working day.

## 5 Getting Help
For IT problems, contact the IT Service Desk (extension 4357, servicedesk.company.com). For HR questions, use the HR Portal or hr-helpdesk@company.com. For payroll questions, email payroll@company.com. For facilities and building access, email facilities@company.com.
