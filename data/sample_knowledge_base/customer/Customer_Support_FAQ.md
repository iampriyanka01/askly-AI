# Customer Support FAQ

Answers to the questions customers ask most often about their Acme Cloud account, billing and data. Our support team is available by in-app chat and at support@company.com, Monday to Friday 08:00 to 20:00 (customer's local time). Enterprise plan customers also have 24/7 phone support.

## 1 Account

### 1.1 Creating an Account
Sign up at app.company.com/signup with your work email. You will receive a verification email; click the link within 24 hours to activate the account. A 14-day free trial of the Professional plan starts automatically.

### 1.2 Changing the Account Email Address
Go to Settings > Profile > Email, enter the new address and confirm it with the verification link sent to that address. For security, a notification is also sent to the old address.

### 1.3 Adding and Removing Users
Account administrators can invite users under Settings > Team > Invite Users. Removing a user frees up their seat immediately; their content is transferred to the administrator.

### 1.4 Deleting an Account
Account administrators can delete the account under Settings > Account > Delete Account. All data is permanently deleted after a 30-day grace period, during which the account can be restored by contacting support.

## 2 Billing and Plans

### 2.1 Plans and Pricing
Acme Cloud has three plans: Starter (USD 12 per user per month), Professional (USD 29 per user per month) and Enterprise (custom pricing, contact sales@company.com). Annual billing gives a 20% discount.

### 2.2 Changing Your Plan
Upgrade or downgrade under Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Upgrades take effect immediately and are charged pro-rata. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period.

### 2.3 Payment Methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and, for annual Enterprise plans, bank transfer against invoice. Update your card under Settings > Billing > Payment Method.

### 2.4 Downloading Invoices
Invoices are emailed to the billing contact each billing period and can be downloaded as PDF from Settings > Billing > Invoices.

### 2.5 Refund Policy
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time but are not refunded for the current month. Annual plans cancelled within 30 days of purchase or renewal receive a full refund; after 30 days, no refund is given for the remaining term. To request a refund, contact support@company.com with your account ID.

### 2.6 Failed Payments
If a payment fails, we retry it after 3, 5 and 7 days and email the billing contact each time. If all retries fail, the account is moved to read-only mode until the payment method is updated.

## 3 Data and Privacy

### 3.1 Exporting Your Data
Administrators can export all workspace data as CSV or JSON under Settings > Data > Export. Large exports are prepared in the background and a download link is emailed when ready (usually within 1 hour). The link is valid for 7 days.

### 3.2 Where Is My Data Stored?
Customer data is stored in the region chosen at sign-up: United States (Virginia), European Union (Frankfurt) or Asia-Pacific (Singapore). Enterprise customers can request a region migration.

### 3.3 Security Certifications
Acme Cloud is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified. Reports are available to customers under NDA from the Trust Center at trust.company.com.

## 4 Troubleshooting

### 4.1 I Can't Log In
First, try resetting your password from the Login Page using "Forgot Password". If your account uses single sign-on (SSO) through your company, contact your own IT administrator instead. Accounts lock for 15 minutes after 5 failed attempts.

### 4.2 The App Is Slow or Not Loading
Check status.company.com for incidents. Clear your browser cache, disable browser extensions, and try a supported browser (latest Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). If the problem continues, contact support with a screenshot and the time it happened.
