## Executive Summary

Orion Digital Services maintains a global customer success organization serving 500+ enterprise clients across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. Our support operations are designed to deliver exceptional customer experiences while maintaining operational excellence and regulatory compliance. This manual establishes standardized procedures, service level agreements, and quality metrics that ensure consistent service delivery across all regions and customer segments.

The Customer Success organization operates through three primary channels: Technical Support (Tier 1-3), Customer Success Management (CSM), and Technical Account Management (TAM). All customer-facing personnel are required to complete mandatory training in customer service excellence, technical competencies, and compliance protocols before engaging with clients.

**Service Philosophy**: We measure success by customer outcomes, not just issue resolution. Our approach emphasizes proactive engagement, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement to drive customer satisfaction scores above 4.5/5.0 and Net Promoter Scores above 70.

### Quality Metrics & Performance Standards

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
- **First Call Resolution Rate**: 78% target (industry benchmark: 70%)
- **Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)**: 4.6/5.0 target (industry benchmark: 4.2/5.0)
- **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**: 72+ target (industry benchmark: 50)

**Quality Assurance Framework**
All support interactions are subject to quality monitoring through our QA program. Supervisors must ensure that 100% of new agents receive weekly coaching sessions for their first 90 days, with monthly reviews thereafter. Quality scores below 85% trigger mandatory retraining programs.

**Customer Health Scoring**
Our Customer Health Index (CHI) combines multiple factors:
- **Usage Metrics**: 30% weight (login frequency, feature adoption, data volume)
- **Support Metrics**: 25% weight (ticket volume, resolution time, satisfaction)
- **Engagement Metrics**: 25% weight (CSM meetings, training attendance, feedback)
- **Business Metrics**: 20% weight (contract renewal likelihood, expansion opportunities)

Health scores below 60 trigger proactive intervention by Customer Success Managers within 48 hours.

## Issue Classification & Escalation Procedures

### Priority Classification Matrix

**Critical (P1) - Business Impact: Severe**
- Complete system outage affecting all users
- Security breach or data exposure
- Data loss or corruption
- Service unavailable to all customers
- Compliance violation requiring immediate action
- **Response Time**: 15 minutes (Platinum), 30 minutes (Gold), 1 hour (Silver/Bronze)
- **Escalation**: Immediate notification to VP of Customer Success and CTO

**High (P2) - Business Impact: Significant**
- Major feature malfunction affecting >50% of users
- Performance degradation >50% below baseline
- Service unavailable to multiple customer environments
- Billing system issues preventing payments
- Integration failures affecting critical workflows
- **Response Time**: 30 minutes (Platinum), 1 hour (Gold), 4 hours (Silver/Bronze)
- **Escalation**: Manager-level notification within 2 hours

**Medium (P3) - Business Impact: Moderate**
- Minor feature issues affecting <50% of users
- Individual customer environment problems
- Configuration questions requiring expert guidance
- General technical inquiries
- Non-critical integration issues
- **Response Time**: 2 hours (Platinum), 4 hours (Gold), 24 hours (Silver/Bronze)
- **Escalation**: Standard escalation procedures

**Low (P4) - Business Impact: Minimal**
- Enhancement requests and feature suggestions
- Documentation questions and training requests
- General information inquiries
- Non-urgent configuration changes
- Cosmetic UI issues
- **Response Time**: 8 hours (Platinum), 24 hours (Gold), 48 hours (Silver/Bronze)
- **Escalation**: Next business day escalation

### Escalation Management Framework

**Level 1 Support (Tier 1)**
- **Scope**: Basic troubleshooting, account questions, password resets, general inquiries
- **Resolution Target**: 80% within 24 hours
- **Escalation Triggers**: 
  - Issue unresolved after 2 documented resolution attempts
  - Customer requests escalation to technical specialist
  - Technical complexity beyond Tier 1 scope
  - Billing disputes exceeding $1,000
  - Security-related concerns

**Level 2 Support (Tier 2)**
- **Scope**: Advanced technical issues, complex integrations, performance optimization
- **Resolution Target**: 90% within 48 hours
- **Escalation Triggers**:
  - Issue requires development team involvement
  - Customer threatens contract cancellation
  - Legal or compliance implications
  - System architecture changes required
  - Multi-customer impact identified

**Level 3 Support (Tier 3)**
- **Scope**: Engineering team, product management, architectural decisions
- **Resolution Target**: Varies based on complexity and development cycles
- **Escalation Triggers**:
  - Bug fixes requiring code changes
  - Feature development requests
  - System architecture modifications
  - Third-party vendor coordination required
  - Custom development work needed

**Executive Escalation**
- **Scope**: C-level executives, VP of Customer Success, Legal Counsel
- **Response Time**: 2 hours for critical issues, 24 hours for high priority
- **Escalation Triggers**:
  - Customer threatens legal action
  - Media attention or public relations concerns
  - Major contract disputes (>$100K impact)
  - Strategic partnership issues
  - Regulatory compliance violations

## Advanced Troubleshooting Procedures

### Complex Technical Issues

#### Database Connectivity Problems
1. **Initial Assessment**
   - Check database server status
   - Verify network connectivity
   - Review error logs for specific error codes
   - Test connection from different environments

2. **Diagnostic Steps**
   ```bash
   # Test database connectivity
   telnet database-server 5432
   
   # Check database processes
   ps aux | grep postgres
   
   # Review connection logs
   tail -f /var/log/postgresql/postgresql.log
   ```

3. **Resolution Procedures**
   - Restart database service if needed
   - Check connection pool settings
   - Verify firewall rules
   - Update database drivers if necessary

#### Performance Issues
1. **Performance Monitoring**
   - Check system resource usage (CPU, memory, disk)
   - Review application response times
   - Analyze database query performance
   - Monitor network latency

2. **Common Performance Bottlenecks**
   - Slow database queries
   - Insufficient memory allocation
   - Network bandwidth limitations
   - Inefficient code algorithms

3. **Performance Optimization**
   - Database query optimization
   - Caching implementation
   - Load balancing configuration
   - Code profiling and optimization

### Customer Health Monitoring

#### Health Score Components
- **Support Ticket Volume**: Frequency of support requests
- **Resolution Time**: Average time to resolve issues
- **Customer Satisfaction**: CSAT scores and feedback
- **Product Usage**: Feature adoption and engagement
- **Payment History**: On-time payment and contract renewal

#### Health Score Calculation
```
Health Score = (Usage Score × 0.3) + (Satisfaction Score × 0.3) + 
               (Support Score × 0.2) + (Payment Score × 0.2)

Where:
- Usage Score: Based on feature adoption and engagement
- Satisfaction Score: Based on CSAT and NPS ratings
- Support Score: Based on ticket volume and resolution time
- Payment Score: Based on payment history and contract renewal
```

#### Intervention Triggers
- **Red Alert**: Health score < 30, immediate intervention required
- **Yellow Alert**: Health score 30-60, proactive outreach needed
- **Green Status**: Health score > 60, maintain current service level
