## Executive Summary

Orion Digital Services maintains a comprehensive human resources organization supporting 800+ employees across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. Our HR operations are designed to attract, develop, and retain exceptional talent while ensuring compliance with employment laws and fostering an inclusive, high-performance culture. This manual establishes standardized policies, procedures, and practices that ensure consistent HR service delivery across all regions.

The Human Resources organization operates through five primary domains: Talent Acquisition & Onboarding, Employee Relations & Engagement, Compensation & Benefits, Learning & Development, and HR Operations & Compliance. All HR personnel are required to complete mandatory training in employment law, diversity & inclusion, and HR best practices before supporting employees or managers.

**HR Philosophy**: We prioritize employee experience, career development, and organizational effectiveness while maintaining fairness, compliance, and business results. Our approach emphasizes data-driven decisions, continuous improvement, and stakeholder engagement to deliver exceptional HR services.

## Talent Acquisition & Onboarding

### Recruitment Strategy & Process

**Recruitment Philosophy**
- **Merit-Based Hiring**: Decisions based on qualifications, skills, and cultural fit
- **Diversity & Inclusion**: Proactive outreach to underrepresented groups
- **Candidate Experience**: Respectful, transparent, and efficient process
- **Internal Mobility**: Priority consideration for internal candidates
- **Employer Branding**: Consistent representation of company culture and values

**Recruitment Process**
1. **Job Requisition**: Manager submits detailed job description and requirements
2. **Sourcing**: Multi-channel approach (job boards, referrals, social media, agencies)
3. **Screening**: Phone/video screening with HR and hiring manager
4. **Interview Process**: Structured interviews with multiple stakeholders
5. **Assessment**: Skills testing and behavioral assessments as needed
6. **Reference Checks**: Professional references and background verification
7. **Offer**: Competitive offer with clear terms and conditions
8. **Onboarding**: Comprehensive 90-day onboarding program

**Interview Standards**
- **Panel Interviews**: Minimum 2 interviewers for all positions
- **Structured Questions**: Consistent questions based on job requirements
- **Behavioral Interviews**: STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
- **Cultural Fit**: Assessment of alignment with company values
- **Documentation**: Complete interview notes and evaluation forms

### Onboarding Excellence

**Pre-Boarding (Week -2 to 0)**
- **Offer Acceptance**: Electronic offer letter and acceptance
- **Background Check**: Third-party verification (Sterling)
- **Equipment Setup**: IT provisioning and device preparation
- **Welcome Package**: Company information and first-day schedule
- **Manager Preparation**: Onboarding checklist and first-week plan

**First Day Experience**
- **Welcome Session**: HR orientation and company overview
- **IT Setup**: Account creation, device provisioning, system access
- **Manager Meeting**: Role expectations and team introduction
- **Workspace Setup**: Desk assignment, supplies, and workspace tour
- **Lunch**: Team lunch with manager and colleagues

**30-60-90 Day Plan**
- **30 Days**: Role mastery, system training, team integration
- **60 Days**: Project assignments, skill development, feedback sessions
- **90 Days**: Performance review, goal setting, career planning
- **Check-ins**: Weekly manager meetings, monthly HR check-ins
- **Feedback**: 360-degree feedback at 90 days

## Employee Relations & Engagement

### Performance Management

**Performance Review Cycle**
- **Annual Reviews**: Comprehensive performance evaluation
- **Mid-Year Reviews**: Progress check and goal adjustment
- **Quarterly Check-ins**: Informal performance discussions
- **Goal Setting**: SMART goals aligned with business objectives
- **Development Planning**: Individual development plans (IDPs)

**Performance Rating Scale**
- **Exceeds Expectations (5)**: Exceptional performance, exceeds all goals
- **Meets Expectations (4)**: Solid performance, meets most goals
- **Partially Meets Expectations (3)**: Adequate performance, some goals met
- **Below Expectations (2)**: Performance improvement needed
- **Unsatisfactory (1)**: Performance significantly below expectations

**Performance Improvement Process**
1. **Identification**: Manager identifies performance gaps
2. **Documentation**: Specific examples and improvement areas
3. **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)**: 60-90 day improvement plan
4. **Support**: Resources, training, and regular feedback
5. **Evaluation**: Assessment of improvement progress
6. **Decision**: Continued employment or separation

### Employee Engagement & Culture

**Engagement Survey Program**
- **Annual Survey**: Comprehensive engagement assessment
- **Pulse Surveys**: Quarterly quick check-ins
- **Exit Interviews**: Structured departure interviews
- **Stay Interviews**: Proactive retention conversations
- **Action Planning**: Department-specific improvement plans

**Culture & Values**
- **Core Values**: Innovation, Integrity, Collaboration, Excellence
- **Cultural Programs**: Recognition programs, team building, volunteerism
- **Diversity & Inclusion**: ERGs, mentorship, unconscious bias training
- **Work-Life Balance**: Flexible work, wellness programs, mental health support
- **Communication**: Town halls, all-hands meetings, feedback channels

## Compensation & Benefits

### Compensation Philosophy

**Compensation Strategy**
- **Market Competitive**: Pay at 50th-60th percentile for fully proficient performance
- **Performance-Based**: Merit increases and variable compensation
- **Equity Focus**: Stock options and RSUs for all employees
- **Total Rewards**: Comprehensive compensation and benefits package
- **Transparency**: Clear communication about compensation philosophy

**Salary Administration**
- **Job Evaluation**: Market pricing and internal equity analysis
- **Salary Bands**: Transparent pay ranges by job family and level
- **Merit Increases**: Annual salary reviews based on performance
- **Promotions**: Salary adjustments for role changes
- **Market Adjustments**: Periodic market-based salary updates

**Variable Compensation**
- **Annual Bonus**: Performance-based bonus program
- **Sales Commission**: Commission structure for sales roles
- **Project Bonuses**: Special recognition for exceptional projects
- **Retention Bonuses**: Targeted retention for critical roles
- **Equity Programs**: Stock options, RSUs, and ESPP

### Benefits Administration

**Health & Wellness Benefits**
- **Medical Insurance**: Company pays 75% of employee premiums
- **Dental Insurance**: Company pays 50% of employee premiums
- **Vision Insurance**: Company pays 100% of employee premiums
- **Mental Health**: EAP and mental health coverage
- **Wellness Programs**: Gym membership, health screenings, nutrition counseling

**Financial Benefits**
- **401(k) Plan**: 4% company match, immediate vesting
- **Health Savings Account**: Company contribution up to $800/year
- **Life Insurance**: 1x annual salary, buy-up options available
- **Disability Insurance**: Short-term and long-term disability coverage
- **Educational Assistance**: Up to $3,000/year for approved programs

**Time Off & Work-Life Balance**
- **Paid Time Off**: 15-25 days based on tenure
- **Sick Leave**: 10 days per year
- **Parental Leave**: 12 weeks paid for birthing parents, 6 weeks for non-birthing
- **Bereavement Leave**: 3 days for immediate family, 1 day for extended
- **Jury Duty**: Paid time off for jury service
- **Volunteer Time**: 2 days per year for community service
