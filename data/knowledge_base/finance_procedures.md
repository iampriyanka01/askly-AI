## Executive Summary

Orion Digital Services maintains a comprehensive finance organization supporting $150M+ annual revenue across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. Our finance operations are designed to ensure accurate financial reporting, regulatory compliance, and strategic financial management while maintaining the highest standards of internal controls and audit readiness. This manual establishes standardized procedures, accounting policies, and financial controls that ensure consistent financial management across all regions.

The Finance organization operates through five primary domains: Financial Reporting & Accounting, Treasury & Cash Management, Tax Compliance & Planning, Internal Audit & Controls, and Strategic Financial Planning. All finance personnel are required to complete mandatory training in accounting standards, regulatory requirements, and internal controls before accessing financial systems or processing transactions.

**Finance Philosophy**: We prioritize accuracy, transparency, and compliance while maintaining operational efficiency and strategic insight. Our approach emphasizes strong internal controls, automated processes, and continuous improvement to deliver exceptional financial management and reporting.

### Accounting Standards & Policies

**Revenue Recognition (ASC 606)**
- **Software Licenses**: Recognized upon delivery and customer acceptance
- **Professional Services**: Recognized over time using percentage-of-completion
- **Subscriptions**: Recognized ratably over subscription period
- **Support Services**: Recognized ratably over support period
- **Multi-element Arrangements**: Allocated based on standalone selling prices

**Expense Recognition**
- **Accrual Basis**: All expenses recognized when incurred, not when paid
- **Matching Principle**: Expenses matched to related revenues
- **Materiality Threshold**: Items >$5,000 require capitalization
- **Depreciation**: Straight-line method over useful life
- **Amortization**: Intangible assets amortized over useful life

**Foreign Currency Translation**
- **Functional Currency**: USD for US operations, local currency for international
- **Translation Method**: Current rate method for foreign subsidiaries
- **Hedging**: Forward contracts for significant foreign currency exposure
- **Gains/Losses**: Recognized in other comprehensive income

## Internal Controls & SOX Compliance

### Segregation of Duties Framework

**Accounts Payable Process**
- **Invoice Receipt**: Accounts Payable Clerk (cannot approve or pay)
- **Invoice Approval**: Department Manager (cannot process payments)
- **Payment Processing**: Accounts Payable Manager (cannot approve invoices)
- **Bank Reconciliation**: Accounting Manager (cannot process payments)
- **Vendor Master File**: Treasury Manager (separate from payment processing)

**Revenue Recognition Process**
- **Contract Review**: Legal Department (contract terms and conditions)
- **Revenue Calculation**: Revenue Accounting Manager (technical accounting)
- **Revenue Approval**: Controller (final review and approval)
- **Cash Application**: Cash Management Team (separate from recognition)
- **Customer Billing**: Billing Manager (separate from recognition)

**Authorization Limits**
- **Petty Cash**: $500 maximum per transaction
- **Office Supplies**: $2,500 maximum per transaction
- **Travel Expenses**: $5,000 maximum per trip
- **Equipment Purchases**: $25,000 maximum per transaction
- **Contract Services**: $100,000 maximum per contract
- **Capital Expenditures**: Board approval required for >$500K

### SOX Compliance Requirements

**Management Assessment**
- **Annual Assessment**: CEO and CFO certification of internal controls
- **Quarterly Reviews**: Management review of control effectiveness
- **Material Weaknesses**: Immediate disclosure and remediation plan
- **Significant Deficiencies**: Disclosure in management report
- **Control Testing**: Annual testing of key controls

**Documentation Requirements**
- **Process Documentation**: Detailed process flows and procedures
- **Control Matrices**: Risk and control documentation
- **Test Plans**: Annual testing procedures and results
- **Remediation Plans**: Corrective actions for control deficiencies
- **Management Certifications**: Quarterly and annual certifications

## Treasury & Cash Management

### Cash Management Strategy

**Cash Position Management**
- **Target Cash Balance**: $25M minimum operating cash
- **Investment Policy**: Conservative investments with high credit ratings
- **Cash Forecasting**: 13-week rolling cash flow forecast
- **Liquidity Management**: Maintain 90-day operating expense coverage
- **Banking Relationships**: Primary banking with JPMorgan Chase

**Investment Guidelines**
- **Permitted Investments**: US Treasury bills, money market funds, CDs
- **Credit Ratings**: Minimum A-1/P-1 rating for money market investments
- **Maturity Limits**: Maximum 90 days for money market investments
- **Concentration Limits**: Maximum 10% in any single issuer
- **Approval Authority**: CFO approval for investments >$5M

### Working Capital Management

**Accounts Receivable Management**
- **Credit Policy**: Net 30 terms with credit limits based on financial analysis
- **Collection Procedures**: 
  - 15 days: Automated reminder email
  - 30 days: Personal phone call from account manager
  - 45 days: Formal demand letter
  - 60 days: Collection agency referral
- **Bad Debt Provision**: 2% of gross receivables based on historical analysis
- **Aging Analysis**: Weekly aging reports with monthly collection meetings

**Accounts Payable Management**
- **Payment Terms**: Net 30 days standard, negotiate better terms when possible
- **Early Payment Discounts**: Take 2/10 net 30 discounts when cost-effective
- **Vendor Management**: Quarterly vendor performance reviews
- **Payment Processing**: Automated payment runs twice weekly
- **Dispute Resolution**: 48-hour response time for vendor disputes
