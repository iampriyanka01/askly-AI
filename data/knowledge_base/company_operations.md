## Executive Summary

Orion Digital Services operates a global network of facilities supporting 800+ employees across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. Our operations are designed to maintain the highest standards of safety, efficiency, and environmental responsibility while ensuring business continuity and regulatory compliance. This manual establishes standardized procedures, safety protocols, and operational excellence frameworks that ensure consistent service delivery across all regions.

The Operations organization operates through five primary domains: Facilities Management, Security Operations, Procurement & Vendor Management, Environmental Health & Safety, and Business Continuity Planning. All operational personnel are required to complete mandatory training in safety protocols, emergency procedures, and compliance requirements before accessing facilities or managing operations.

**Operations Philosophy**: We prioritize safety, sustainability, and operational excellence while maintaining cost efficiency and regulatory compliance. Our approach emphasizes proactive maintenance, continuous improvement, and stakeholder engagement to deliver exceptional workplace experiences.

### Detailed Emergency Procedures

#### Fire Emergency Response
1. **Immediate Actions**
   - Pull nearest fire alarm
   - Call 911 and provide exact location
   - Evacuate building using nearest exit
   - Do not use elevators
   - Close doors behind you (do not lock)
   - Meet at designated assembly point (parking lot)

2. **Evacuation Procedures**
   - Follow evacuation routes posted in each area
   - Assist disabled persons if safe to do so
   - Account for all personnel at assembly point
   - Do not re-enter building until cleared by fire department
   - Report missing persons to emergency responders

3. **Post-Evacuation Actions**
   - Designated personnel contact emergency services
   - Notify senior management
   - Coordinate with fire department
   - Document incident details
   - Arrange temporary workspace if needed

#### Medical Emergency Response
1. **Immediate Response**
   - Call 911 for serious injuries or medical emergencies
   - Provide first aid if trained and safe to do so
   - Stay with injured person until help arrives
   - Clear area of hazards if possible
   - Notify security and management

2. **First Aid Procedures**
   - **Bleeding**: Apply direct pressure with clean cloth
   - **Burns**: Cool with running water, do not apply ice
   - **Choking**: Perform Heimlich maneuver if trained
   - **Unconscious**: Check breathing, begin CPR if trained
   - **Shock**: Keep person warm and lying down

3. **Documentation Requirements**
   - Complete incident report within 24 hours
   - Notify workers' compensation if work-related
   - Document witness statements
   - Photograph scene if appropriate
   - Maintain confidentiality of medical information

#### Security Threat Response
1. **Active Shooter Protocol**
   - **RUN**: If safe to do so, run to nearest exit
   - **HIDE**: If cannot run, hide in locked room
   - **FIGHT**: As last resort, fight back with improvised weapons
   - Call 911 when safe to do so
   - Provide detailed description of suspect
   - Follow law enforcement instructions

2. **Bomb Threat Procedures**
   - Remain calm and listen carefully
   - Ask questions: location, time, type of device
   - Note caller's voice characteristics
   - Do not hang up phone
   - Notify security immediately
   - Evacuate if instructed by authorities

3. **Suspicious Package Protocol**
   - Do not touch or move package
   - Evacuate immediate area
   - Call 911 and security
   - Do not use radio or cell phones near package
   - Wait for bomb squad clearance
